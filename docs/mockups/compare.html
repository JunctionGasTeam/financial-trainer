Wording rules

  • Position and group, never a verdict. "More than 83% of people your age in the Espoo region", not "you overspend".
  • Group named every time: size, age band and area, so a small group reads as small.
  • Area figures stand alone. They are yearly and before tax, so they never sit next to the user's own amounts in a calculation.
