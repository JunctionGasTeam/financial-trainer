S-Bank challenge · Junction 2026 · Step 3

Baskets

The three baskets open to categories, then merchant types, then single purchases. Anything can be moved at any level, and moved items stay marked until the user resets.

Drill-down

  • Three levels: basket, then a segmented switch between categories, place types and purchases.
  • Row tap drills down, the ⋮ button moves. They never share a target.
  • Breadcrumb on every level below the top, so the basket is always named.
  • Period follows the top switch: this month, usual month, or the last six months.

Moving things

  • Most specific wins: a purchase override beats a place-type override, which beats a category override (SPEC §5).
  • Every figure updates at once, including safe-to-spend on Home.
  • Reset asks for confirmation inside a sheet ("Undo all 1 change?"), never a system dialog.
